AED Route Hamburg — How to use

🗺️
Explore the map
Red dots mark all AED (defibrillator) locations across Hamburg. Toggle their visibility using the Layers panel.
📍
Find the nearest AED
Click anywhere on the map to calculate the shortest route from that point to the nearest AED.
🚶
Choose your transport mode
Select Walk or Bike in the Transport panel before clicking. Each mode uses different road rules and speeds. Car is temporarily unavailable.
🔀
Compare alternative routes
After a route is calculated, click "Compare routes" to see up to 2 alternative AED options with their time difference.
⏱️
Isochrones
Enable the Isochrones layer to see 2-minute and 4-minute walking coverage areas around each AED.
